# Surface: Home and site shell

Scope: the home page (Persuade) and the shared shell every route inherits. Tests and Packages are Operate surfaces inside this world; Home Collection, About and Contact are Read.

Audience and job: nearby patients, often elderly, on low-end Android, half reading Malayalam. Job: know what the lab is, where, how to call or WhatsApp, what the current offer is, in two seconds.
Proof/content: only the verified brief content. Constraints: no invented facts, cardiovascular notice always visible, unconfirmed facts render as "Call us to confirm".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the request a patient will send. Every path ends in a visible, pre-written WhatsApp message on a paper slip, or a tap-to-call number. It refuses the clinic template: stock-photo hero, three service cards, appointment modal.

OWN-WORLD: Deep logo-teal field with one sweeping letterhead arc at its lower edge; cool white paper ground; charcoal ink; petrol blue only for packages and offers. Anek set large in both scripts, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading. The Request Slip: a white note with a teal margin rule quoting the exact message. Ruled lists, not cards; tabular numerals.

STORY: The visitor sees Janasevana Lab at Kaichoondy Jn., reads the offer in plain facts, sees the message already written for them, and taps Call or sends it.
